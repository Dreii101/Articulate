mpv's LGPL configuration and FFmpeg's version3 configuration require reading
the included LGPL/GPL texts together. Full copyright and attribution reside
in their source trees. Native libraries are unmodified publisher binaries.
Nothing in Articulate's terms restricts modification or reverse engineering
needed to debug modifications to the LGPL libraries. Windows uses a separate,
replaceable libmpv-2.dll; the source project permits rebuilding Android with
a replacement library and signing that build with your own key.
